Shihan Wang

Assistant Professor  ·  Utrecht University

I am a Tenured Assistant Professor at the Department of Information & Computing Sciences of Utrecht University, where I am part of the Intelligent Systems Lab. Before joining Utrecht, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Machine Learning Lab (AMLab), University of Amsterdam (2017–2020). I hold a PhD in Computational Intelligence & Systems Science from Terano Lab, Tokyo Institute of Technology (2014–2017), during which I was a Visiting Researcher in Computational Social Science at ETH Zurich (2016–2017). I also hold an MSc in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Edinburgh and a BEng in Software Engineering from Northeastern University, China.

Research Focus

My research concentrates on single-agent and multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) and their practical usage in sequential decision-making tasks. My primary interest balances theoretical development and practical application, with an emphasis on developing efficient, scalable, and reliable algorithms to solve real-world problems. My overarching goal is to develop human-centred & intelligent systems.

My work currently spans three directions:

1  Multi-Agent RL with Communication

How can multiple agents learn to communicate and coordinate effectively? [JAAMAS 24, RLC 26, AAMAS 23]

2  Neuro-Symbolic (MA)RL

How can we incorporate human constraints or domain knowledge into reinforcement learning for safer, more interpretable, and more data-efficient policies? [AAMAS 26, ECAI 2024]

3  RL for Human-Centred LLMs

How can reinforcement learning support large language models to better align with human preferences for conversational human–AI interaction and collaboration? [EMNLP 26, EMNLP 25, TACL 24]

Among various application domains, I am particularly interested in healthcare, where I am currently involved in several ongoing projects.
